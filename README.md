Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Cars Dashboard 🚗
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📊 Project Overview
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This interactive dashboard is designed for the insurance industry to provide deep insights into policy portfolios and claim patterns across multiple dimensions, including age groups, car types, coverage zones, education levels, and marital status.

🔍 Key Features
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
◾ Comprehensive breakdown of total policies and claim amounts by car type, age group, education, and more.

◾ Visualization of claim frequency and average claim amounts.

◾ Trends in claims and policies by year, identifying shifts and risk factors.

◾ Insights into the impact of child drivers on total claims.

◾ In-depth segmentation for decision-making: zone coverage, gender, and education-marital status combinations.



🛠 Tools Used
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power BI | Dax Measures



🎯 The goals
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With these analytics, insurance professionals can:

◾ Continuously monitor product performance and uncover key risk drivers.

◾ Optimize pricing strategies and risk management using real data.

◾ Foster better decision-making and strategic planning for claims and policy.
